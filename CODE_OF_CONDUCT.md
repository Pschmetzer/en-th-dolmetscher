# Code of Conduct

Dolmetscher is about people who don't share a language understanding each other. We want the project itself to work the same way: friendly, patient and respectful, whatever someone's language, background or experience.

## What we expect

- Be kind and assume good intent. Many contributors write in their second or third language.
- Give feedback on the work, not on the person. Explain what is wrong and, if you can, how to fix it.
- Respect native speakers' corrections of Thai and other texts, even if a machine translation said otherwise.
- Accept that maintainers may say no to a change, and say no politely yourself.
- Protect privacy: no personal data, recordings, logs with conversation text or other people's names in issues and pull requests.

## What we don't accept

- Insults, harassment, threats, or discriminatory jokes and remarks (about nationality, ethnicity, language, accent, gender, sexual orientation, religion, disability, age or anything else).
- Mocking someone's language skills, accent or culture.
- Sexualised language or images.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Repeatedly pushing a topic after being asked to stop.

## Scope

This applies to all project spaces: issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, commit messages and any other place where someone represents the project.

## Reporting

If something happens that breaks these rules, please tell the maintainer privately. You can use the contact on the maintainer's GitHub profile, or open an issue that only says you would like a private contact (no details in public). Reports are handled confidentially.

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, close issues, or block people who break these rules, depending on how serious and repeated the behaviour is. Usually the first step is a private note asking someone to change their behaviour.
